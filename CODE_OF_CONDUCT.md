# Community conduct

ScopeRook's community is for careful, authorized security research. Be direct, respectful, and evidence-led.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and findings without attacking people.
- Separate verified observations from assumptions.
- Respect program scope, privacy, and disclosure timelines.
- Remove secrets and private target information from public discussions.
- Give credit for research and code.
- Accept corrections when the evidence changes.

## Unacceptable conduct

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or deliberate disruption;
- encouraging unauthorized access, destructive testing, credential attacks, or exposure of another person's data;
- posting private bounty-program material, credentials, customer data, or undisclosed vulnerability details;
- misrepresenting automated work, evidence, authorship, acceptance, or financial outcomes.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close participation channels, or ban participants whose conduct harms the project or other people. Report sensitive conduct issues through a private maintainer contact route rather than a public issue.
