About & limits
Where data lives
Engagement JSON and the rebuilt SQLite index live under data/, outside Git. The model server is optional and runs separately on this computer.
What runs
The synthetic demo and owned-lab check each use at most two GET requests. A separate logic sandbox is offline. The AI assistant sends data only to the configured loopback model.
What is planning only
Bounty and internal engagements can hold hypotheses but cannot run checks or launch ZAP, Nuclei, ffuf, or Shannon. A saved plan does not authorize live traffic.
Evidence standard
Lab evidence saves status, body length, and SHA-256 digest, without response bodies or credentials. Model results and AI drafts do not establish a vulnerability.
